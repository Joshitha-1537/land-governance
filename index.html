Problem, Digital Solution & Impact

The platform addresses challenges in land governance by connecting data, research, policy and stakeholder participation.

⚠️

Challenge

Land information and research evidence can be fragmented, making informed land-use planning and governance difficult.

💻

Digital Solution

A unified digital platform connects land data, research, analytics, policy ideas and stakeholder feedback.

🌱

Expected Impact

Better transparency, evidence-based decisions, sustainable planning and stronger stakeholder participation.

Key Benefits

The platform supports better land governance through accessible information, evidence and participation.

🔍

Better Transparency

Improve access to land information and governance evidence.

📊

Evidence-Based Decisions

Use data and research evidence to support informed decisions.

👥

Stakeholder Participation

Provide a channel for stakeholders to share feedback and ideas.

🌱

Sustainable Planning

Support responsible and sustainable land-use planning.
